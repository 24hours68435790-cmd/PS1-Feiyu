\subsection{Mechanism, transcribed from the deployed \texttt{index.html}}
\textbf{Draw (on Reset).} A seeded PRNG (\texttt{mulberry32}, default seed 2026)
picks $\theta\in\{1,2,3\}$ and $k\in\{0.5,1,2\}$. Same seed $\Rightarrow$ same
draw.
\textbf{Actions.} $a\in\{0,1\}$; $m$ from $\{0,1,2\}$ or a typed custom value.
\textbf{Value and report.} $v=\theta+a$; $s=\theta+a+m$; outcome $y=v+\epsilon$
with $\epsilon\in\{-\sigma,0,+\sigma\}$ uniform, revealed only after pricing;
$\sigma$ is a control, default $0.5$ (the Week~2 fixed value).
\textbf{Committed price.} $p=\max(0,\,s-\bar m)$ with announced $\bar m=1$.
\textbf{Revisable price.} Round~1 identical. Round~2:
$p=\max(0,\,s-\bar m-\lambda g_1)$ with $g_1=(s_1-y_1)-\bar m$ and $\lambda$ a
control, default $1$. Because $g_1$ can be negative, the revisable rule can
also price an honest firm \emph{up}.
\textbf{Payoffs.} $\pi=p-0.5a-km^{2}$; $W=v-km^{2}$; mispricing $=|p-v|$. No
other rule is implemented: the Space contains no audit intensity, no CFR solver
and no learning agent.

\subsection{Replication of the browser artifact}
\texttt{strategic\_disclosure.Space} ports \texttt{mulberry32} and
\texttt{Math.imul} to Python as unsigned 32-bit arithmetic.
\texttt{verify\_space\_replication()} asserts, and the notebook fails loudly if
any of these disagree:

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Draw.} Seed 2026 $\Rightarrow$ $\theta=2$, $k=0.5$.
\item \emph{Boundary, revisable} ($a=0$, $m=2$ twice). R1: $v=2.00$, $s=4.00$,
$\epsilon=0.00$, $y=2.00$, price 3.00, $|p-v|=1.00$, $\pi=1.00$,
$g_1=1.00$. R2: price 2.00, $|p-v|=0.00$, $\pi=0.00$.
\item \emph{Boundary, committed control.} Identical seed and actions: 3.00 in
both rounds, $|p-v|=1.00$ in both, $\pi=1.00$ in both. Only the rule differs.
\item \emph{Typical} ($a=1$, $m=0$, an honest firm; $v=3.00$). Committed: 2.00
twice, $|p-v|=1.00$---the announced allowance is deducted from an honest report
too. Revisable: 2.00 then 3.00, carrying $g_1=-1.00$, so $|p-v|$ falls to 0.00.
\item \emph{Intentionally invalid} ($m=7$, then $m=$``abc''). Rejected at input:
no report submitted, round counter unchanged, no state modified---not a clamp,
not a silent default, not an uncaught exception. The Python twin raises
\texttt{ValueError} at the same boundary.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Responsible boundary}
\textbf{Runtime.} Static SDK confirmed: no Hugging Face CPU/GPU application
runtime, no backend, no database, no secret, no API call, no paid service.
\emph{This is not a zero-impact claim:} hosting, network transfer and
client-side CPU are still consumed, so the claim is the narrower, verifiable one
that no dedicated application runtime is provisioned.
\textbf{Privacy.} No personal data collected, stored or transmitted; no cookies,
no analytics, no real firm data---every quantity is simulated.
\textbf{Accessibility.} Colour contrast was computed for every
foreground/background pair in the Space's stylesheet and meets WCAG~AA
throughout (lowest pair 7.2:1 against a 4.5:1 minimum); result updates are
announced through an \texttt{aria-live} region; every interactive control has a
visible keyboard focus ring; the page reflows at 200\% zoom without horizontal
scrolling.
\textbf{What remains unverified.} The behavioural cost distribution has not been
elicited from human subjects; the discretised grid has not been shown to
preserve the equilibrium of a continuous game; the revisable evaluator has not
been tested against deliberate false-history construction; best responses are
computed against a posted rule rather than as a full equilibrium fixed point;
and no output should be read as a valuation of, or evidence about, any real firm.

\emph{Capability:} runs reproducible, provenance-tracked experiments that
quantify degradation under a declared attack and recovery under a declared
defence---the NIST AI RMF~\cite{nist2023} ``Measure'' function, operationalised.
\emph{Limitation:} its threat model is input perturbation with no attacker cost
or payoff, so it measures robustness rather than equilibrium and cannot say
whether a rational firm would \emph{pay} to game a rule; it also needs a server.
\emph{Design consequence:} that missing cost layer names my gap rather than a
certification capability, and it is why Section~3 reports deviation gain as a
first-class metric.

\noindent\textbf{Two-sector value.} Privately, the protocol becomes a
pre-deployment test of whether a scoring rule rewards genuine quality or
strategically cheap reporting. Publicly, it becomes a traceable measurement
record an external evaluator can rerun without the model's weights.

\subsection{Reviewer 1 --- Aaron Wang}
\textbf{Appreciated.} ``The research question and three hypotheses are very
clear.'' \emph{Retained} --- Sections~1--2 keep the question and H1--H3 verbatim,
with the evidence status now printed on Figure~\ref{fig:teaser} itself.

\textbf{Constructive concern.} ``The effect of noise should be tested more
carefully$\ldots$ Test the model under different levels of outcome noise.''
\emph{Addressed, Section~3 and Figure~\ref{fig:noise}.} $\sigma$ swept over
$\{0,0.25,\ldots,3.0\}$ with 20{,}000 draws per cell. Result: deviation gain is
flat in $\sigma$ (the deterrence result survives), while accuracy under the
revisable rule degrades monotonically. \emph{Verification:}
\texttt{companion/results/sweep\_noise.csv}, regenerated by
\texttt{python companion/src/run\_experiments.py}.

\textbf{Question.} ``Can memory sometimes incorrectly punish an honest firm?''
\emph{Answered, Section~4.} Yes---an honest firm is priced below value in
$49.9\%$ of draws for any $\sigma>0$---but the comparison that matters has a
threshold: it is worse than commitment only when $\sigma>\bar m$, with share
$(\sigma-\bar m)/\sigma$, and worse on average only when $\sigma>2\bar m$. The
analytic derivation and the simulation agree to three decimals
(\texttt{companion/results/false\_punishment.csv}). Aaron Wang's question produced the
sharpest single result in the paper, and it is the quantity Section~5 now
identifies as what a regulator would need.

\emph{Retained} --- the three-layer structure is unchanged, though each layer is

\textbf{Constructive concern.} ``Can test more $\ldots$ and memory strength

\textbf{Concern, partly deferred.} ``Can be stronger with validation across

Table~\ref{tab:roadmap} rather than claimed here. What I could do within PS1 is
the closest available analogue---varying the announced allowance $\bar m$ and
the noise $\sigma$, which together are the institutional parameters that move
the false-punishment threshold---and that is reported in Section~4.
